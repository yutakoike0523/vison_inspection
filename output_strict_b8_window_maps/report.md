## Strict B8 radial-FFT maps

Each block is mean-subtracted, Hann-windowed, and transformed with rFFT2. Power is normalized by total in-band power then radial-bin averaged; log10 power spectra over 0.01–0.50 cycles/pixel are compared. Score = RMS(target spectrum − mean of 8 reference spectra). Only all-8-reference locations are valid.
