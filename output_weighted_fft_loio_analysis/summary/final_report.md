# Weighted low-frequency FFT LOIO analysis

Scores reuse the fixed-K=4, 256 px, stride-16 radial-FFT spectra. No FFT was recomputed.

Evaluation: 15 deformation instances in images 2–17; LOIO threshold is the equal-image-weighted 99th percentile of normal windows.

Best tested alpha: 0.85; detected 12/15; mean normal exceedance 1.06%.
